# Test prompts for agent/orchestrator.py - one question per line, blank
# lines and '#' comments are ignored. Run with:
#   python orchestrator.py --batch prompts.txt --show-trace
#
# Each run is independent (no memory) - for exercising multi-turn memory,
# use the REPL instead. These lean toward questions that need chaining wiki
# knowledge with player state, which is the whole point of this loop over
# the router's single-pass design - see modules/orchestrator_prompts.txt for
# the router's own comparison set.

# --- the worked example this design was built around ---
I am hungry, do I have any food left?

# --- crafting chains (may need skill_crafting_chain) ---
Can I make a diamond sword right now with what I have?
Do I have everything I need to build a beacon?

# --- cross-referencing wiki knowledge with inventory/containers ---
What's the most valuable item I'm currently carrying?
What can I do with the items from my inventory?
Do I have any potion effects? If yes, what does it do and when does it run out?

# --- navigation (may need skill_player_navigation) ---
How do I get back to where I died last?
Which of the structures I've found is closest to me right now?

# --- vague/colloquial wiki lookups (may need skill_wiki_search_strategy) ---
What does the black monster in the nether drop?
How do I deal with the flying monster that shoots fireballs?

# --- broad "what exists" questions (may read a seed page) ---
What food items are there and which is best for me right now?
