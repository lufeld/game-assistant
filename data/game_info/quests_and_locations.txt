QUESTS AND LOCATIONS

MAIN QUEST LINE:

Quest: The Lost Artifact
- Level requirement: 1
- Starting location: Village of Greenwood
- Objectives:
  1. Speak to Elder Marcus in Greenwood
  2. Travel to the Ancient Ruins
  3. Defeat the Guardian of the Ruins
  4. Retrieve the Crystal of Power
  5. Return to Elder Marcus
- Rewards: 500 XP, 200 gold, Iron Sword
- Description: The village elder needs help recovering an ancient artifact to protect the village from dark forces.

Quest: The Dragon's Lair
- Level requirement: 15
- Starting location: City of Ironforge
- Objectives:
  1. Gather information about the dragon from locals
  2. Prepare by crafting or purchasing fire-resistant gear
  3. Travel to Dragon Mountain
  4. Navigate through the mountain caves
  5. Defeat the Ancient Dragon
  6. Claim the dragon's treasure
- Rewards: 5000 XP, 2000 gold, Dragon Scale, choice of legendary weapon
- Description: A fearsome dragon has been terrorizing the region. Only a true hero can defeat it.

SIDE QUESTS:

Quest: The Missing Herbs
- Level requirement: 3
- Starting location: Greenwood Village
- Objectives:
  1. Speak to Herbalist Anna
  2. Collect 5 Moonflowers from the Forest
  3. Collect 3 Red Mushrooms from the Cave
  4. Return herbs to Anna
- Rewards: 150 XP, 50 gold, 3 Health Potions
- Description: The village herbalist needs rare ingredients for her remedies.

Quest: Bandit Troubles
- Level requirement: 5
- Starting location: Trading Post
- Objectives:
  1. Talk to Merchant Guild representative
  2. Locate the Bandit Camp in the Western Woods
  3. Defeat Bandit Leader and 5 bandits
  4. Recover stolen goods
  5. Return to Trading Post
- Rewards: 300 XP, 150 gold, Leather Armor
- Description: Bandits have been raiding merchant caravans and must be stopped.

LOCATIONS:

Village of Greenwood
- Type: Starting area
- Level range: 1-5
- Features: Inn, General Store, Blacksmith, Herbalist
- Description: A peaceful village surrounded by forests, perfect for new adventurers.

Ancient Ruins
- Type: Dungeon
- Level range: 3-7
- Enemies: Skeletons, Zombies, Guardian Golem
- Treasures: Ancient coins, Old weapons, Crystal of Power
- Description: Mysterious ruins from a long-lost civilization, now inhabited by undead creatures.

City of Ironforge
- Type: Major city
- Level range: 10+
- Features: Large marketplace, Mage Tower, Training Grounds, Multiple guilds
- Description: A bustling metropolis and center of trade and learning.

Dragon Mountain
- Type: Raid dungeon
- Level range: 15-20
- Enemies: Dragon Cultists, Fire Elementals, Ancient Dragon (boss)
- Treasures: Dragon hoard with legendary items and massive gold
- Description: A treacherous mountain lair where an ancient dragon sleeps atop its treasure.

Whispering Forest
- Type: Exploration area
- Level range: 4-8
- Resources: Herbs, Wood, Wild game for hunting
- Secrets: Hidden groves, Fairy circles, Ancient tree spirits
- Description: A magical forest filled with both beauty and danger.

Dark Caverns
- Type: Mine/Cave system
- Level range: 6-10
- Resources: Ore deposits, Crystals, Rare gems
- Enemies: Giant Spiders, Cave Trolls, Bats
- Description: Deep underground caverns rich in minerals but home to dangerous creatures.
