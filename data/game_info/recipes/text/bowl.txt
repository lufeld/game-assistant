============================================================

Description:
To craft a Bowl in Minecraft, you’ll need 3 planks.  

Place them in a 3x3 crafting grid like this:  
- Put one plank in the top-left corner.  
- Put one plank in the top-right corner.  
- Put one plank in the center of the grid.  

The other spots in the grid can be empty.  

When you arrange it like this, you’ll get 4 Bowls. Just make sure you have 3 planks ready — they can be from any wood type, like oak, spruce, or any other.
