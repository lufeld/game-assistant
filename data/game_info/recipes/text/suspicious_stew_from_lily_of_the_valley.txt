Description:
To make Suspicious Stew in Minecraft, you don’t need to follow a specific pattern—just place the ingredients in any order on a crafting table. You’ll need:

- 1 Bowl  
- 1 Brown Mushroom  
- 1 Red Mushroom  
- 1 Lily of the Valley  

Just drop these four items into the crafting grid, no matter where you put them. The result will be 1 Suspicious Stew. It’s a tasty item that can be used to heal or give you a little extra health when you drink it.
