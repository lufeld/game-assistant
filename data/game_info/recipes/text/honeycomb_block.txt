To craft a Honeycomb Block in Minecraft, you’ll need 4 Honeycomb items.  

Place them in a 2x2 square on your crafting table like this:  
- Top-left corner: one Honeycomb  
- Top-center: one Honeycomb

- Center: one Honeycomb  

Just arrange the four Honeycomb items in a square shape, and the Honeycomb Block will appear in the result slot. It’s a simple 2x2 pattern—easy to remember once you see it!
