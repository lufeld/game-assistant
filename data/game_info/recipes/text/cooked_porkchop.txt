Recipe: Cooked Porkchop
Result: 1x Cooked Porkchop

Recipe Type: Smelting (Furnace)

Input: 1x Porkchop
Processing time: 200 ticks (10.0 seconds)
Experience: 0.35

============================================================

Description:
To make a Cooked Porkchop in Minecraft, you’ll need one Porkchop and a Furnace.

Place the Porkchop in the Furnace. You don’t need to arrange it in a grid or use any special setup—just put the porkchop in the furnace slot, and it will start cooking. It takes about 10 seconds (200 ticks) to finish.

Once it’s done, a Cooked Porkchop will appear in your inventory. This recipe gives you 0.35 experience points, so it’s a small reward for your effort.

Just remember: you can’t make it with a crafting table or by combining items—only by smelting the raw porkchop in a furnace.
