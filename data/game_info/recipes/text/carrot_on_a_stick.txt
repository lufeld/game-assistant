Recipe: Carrot On A Stick
Result: 1x Carrot On A Stick

Recipe Type: Shaped Crafting

Crafting Grid Layout:
  [Fishing Rod] [Empty]
  [Empty] [Carrot]

Ingredients needed:
  - 1x Fishing Rod
  - 1x Carrot

Step-by-step placement:
  1. Place Fishing Rod in top-left
  2. Place Carrot in center

============================================================

Description:
To craft a Carrot on a Stick, you’ll need a Fishing Rod and a Carrot.  

Place the Fishing Rod in the top-left corner of the 3x3 crafting grid. Then, put the Carrot in the center of the grid (the middle slot of the second row).  

That’s it! Once you’ve arranged them like that, the Carrot on a Stick will appear in the result slot. It’s a quick craft—just a few clicks and you’re done.
