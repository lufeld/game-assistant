Description:
To make Cooked Cod in Minecraft, you’ll need 1 Cod and a furnace.  

Place the Cod in the furnace and let it smelt for about 10 seconds (or 200 ticks). The process is simple: just put the raw Cod in the furnace, and after it finishes smelting, you’ll get 1 Cooked Cod.  

This recipe gives you 0.35 experience points, so it’s a small reward for your effort. No crafting table or specific arrangement is needed—just the raw Cod and a working furnace.  

Cooked Cod is great for eating or using in recipes later, like making a stew or a potion.
