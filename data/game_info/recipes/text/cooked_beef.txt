Result: 1x Cooked Beef

Recipe Type: Smelting (Furnace)

Input: 1x Beef
Processing time: 200 ticks (10.0 seconds)

Description:
To make Cooked Beef in Minecraft, you’ll need 1 piece of Beef. Place the Beef in a furnace and smelt it. The process takes about 10 seconds (200 ticks) and gives you 1 Cooked Beef. It doesn’t give any experience points. Just drop the Beef into the furnace and wait for it to finish—no special arrangement needed. The result is a tasty, usable food item.
