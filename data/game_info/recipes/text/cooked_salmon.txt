Recipe: Cooked Salmon
Result: 1x Cooked Salmon

Recipe Type: Smelting (Furnace)

Input: 1x Salmon
Processing time: 200 ticks (10.0 seconds)
Experience: 0.35

============================================================

Description:
To make Cooked Salmon in Minecraft, you’ll need 1 Salmon and a furnace.  

Place the Salmon in the furnace and let it smelt for 10 seconds (or 200 ticks). The process is simple: just put the raw salmon in the furnace, and after it finishes smelting, you’ll get 1 Cooked Salmon.  

This recipe gives you 0.35 experience points, so it’s a small reward, but the result is a tasty food item you can use to restore health. No crafting table or special arrangement is needed—just drop the salmon into the furnace and wait.
