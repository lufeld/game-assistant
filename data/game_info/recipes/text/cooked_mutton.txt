Recipe: Cooked Mutton
Result: 1x Cooked Mutton

Recipe Type: Smelting (Furnace)

Input: 1x Mutton
Processing time: 200 ticks (10.0 seconds)

============================================================

Description:
To make Cooked Mutton in Minecraft, you’ll need 1 Mutton and a furnace.  

Place the Mutton in the furnace and let it smelt for about 10 seconds—this is the processing time. The result will be 1 Cooked Mutton, which gives you 0.35 experience points.  

No crafting table or specific arrangement is needed—just drop the Mutton into the furnace and wait for it to finish. Cooked Mutton is great for eating or using in recipes later.
