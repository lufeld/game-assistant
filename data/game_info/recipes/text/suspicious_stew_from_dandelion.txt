Recipe: Suspicious Stew
Result: 1x Suspicious Stew

Recipe Type: Shapeless Crafting

Ingredients (can be placed in any arrangement):
  - 1x Bowl
  - 1x Brown Mushroom
  - 1x Red Mushroom
  - 1x Dandelion

============================================================

Description:
To craft Suspicious Stew in Minecraft, you don’t need to follow a specific layout—just place any of these four items in a crafting grid, no matter where they go.

You’ll need:
- 1 Bowl  
- 1 Brown Mushroom  
- 1 Red Mushroom  
- 1 Dandelion  

Just drop them into any spot in the 3x3 crafting grid, and as long as you have all four, the recipe will work. The result is one Suspicious Stew, which gives you a chance to get a random item when eaten—like a rare enchantment or a useful tool. It’s a fun, quick way to get a surprise in-game!
