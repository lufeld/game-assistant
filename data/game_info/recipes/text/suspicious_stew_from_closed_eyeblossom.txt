Recipe: Suspicious Stew
Result: 1x Suspicious Stew

Recipe Type: Shapeless Crafting

Ingredients (can be placed in any arrangement):
  - 1x Bowl
  - 1x Brown Mushroom
  - 1x Red Mushroom
  - 1x Closed Eyeblossom

============================================================

Description:
To craft Suspicious Stew in Minecraft, you don’t need to follow a specific pattern — it’s a shapeless recipe, which means you can place the ingredients in any order.

You’ll need:
- 1 Bowl  
- 1 Brown Mushroom  
- 1 Red Mushroom  
- 1 Closed Eyeblossom  

Just open your crafting table and place any three of these items in any spot — the fourth will go in the remaining space. The result will be one Suspicious Stew.  

Note: The Closed Eyeblossom is a rare item, so make sure you have it before trying. This stew can be used to give you a chance to find a rare item when you drink it — it’s a fun little surprise!
