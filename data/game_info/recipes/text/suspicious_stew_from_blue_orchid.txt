Description:
To make Suspicious Stew in Minecraft, you don’t need to follow a specific pattern—just place the ingredients in any arrangement in a crafting grid. You’ll need:

- 1 Bowl  
- 1 Brown Mushroom  
- 1 Red Mushroom  
- 1 Blue Orchid  

Just drop these four items into any spot in the 3x3 crafting grid, and Suspicious Stew will appear in the result slot. It’s a shapeless recipe, so the order doesn’t matter. The stew is a fun, useful item that gives you a small health boost and can be used in various recipes later.
